The serene golden hour magic transforms the Jeju sky, offering a truly captivating jeju sunset time experience. Grande Bleu Yacht invites guests to discover this beauty from the comfort of a certified catamaran, sailing from Daepo Port, Seogwipo. This 60-minute jeju ocean tour provides an unforgettable perspective of Jeju's stunning coastline.

Here’s what guests can expect:
·  A certified catamaran operator in Jeju since 2011, having welcomed over 100,000 guests across two vessels (47-seat and 44-seat).
·  The Sunset course unveils breathtaking coastal features such as Elephant Rock, Jingotnae Sea Canyon, Wolpyeong Basalt Columns, Pangpaengideok, and the Large Sea Cave.
·  Guests enjoy complimentary, unlimited onboard refreshments, including draft beer, wine, Jeju tangerine juice, water, Jeju local snacks, and a satisfying jeju ramyeon cruise experience.
·  For those seeking more exclusivity, a jeju private cruise option is available for an intimate journey.

Weekend slots for this popular experience often fill quickly. Grande Bleu Yacht recommends booking in advance, and weekday cruises can offer a more relaxed atmosphere.
